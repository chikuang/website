Seminar series and local calendars

Biostatistics, bioinformatics, and health data science

  • Emory Biostatistics and Bioinformatics — The BIOS Beat — the department’s biweekly newsletter lists research events and webinars in biostatistics and bioinformatics. Use the Stay In Touch section for current and past issues.
  • Georgia CTSA Biostatistics, Epidemiology, and Research Design (BERD) Research Forum — a recurring forum connecting statistical methods with clinical and translational research across Emory, Georgia Tech, Morehouse School of Medicine, and UGA. Themes have included clinical trials, wearable data, and AI in health care; check the organizer for the next edition.
  • Emory Biomedical Informatics Seminar Series — research talks on clinical prediction, medical imaging, electronic health records, and AI for patient care. The calendar also links to Georgia CTSA informatics lunch-and-learn events; attendance formats vary by session.
  • Emory Tuberculosis Research Advancement Center events — seminars and workshops connecting infectious-disease research with epidemiology, real-world evidence, and AI/ML. Consult individual announcements for local viewing locations and online participation.

Atlanta and nearby Georgia

  • Georgia State Statistics Seminar — statistics talks organized by Yichuan Zhao. The Mathematics and Statistics calendar provides departmental event announcements.
  • Georgia Tech ISyE Statistics Seminar — statistical theory, methods, and applications.
  • Machine Learning at Georgia Tech — current ML seminar and robotics seminar schedules.
  • Georgia Tech IDEaS seminars and events — AI foundations, machine learning, and high-performance computing talks and workshops.
  • Georgia Tech CSIP Seminar — signal processing, statistical learning, and information theory.
  • Emory Data Science lecture series — data science, causal inference, and applications across research disciplines.
  • Emory CODES seminars — computational and data-enabled science, with an associated student scientific-computing seminar.
  • University of Georgia Statistics events — colloquia, lectures, and statistics events in Athens.
  • Atlanta Combinatorics Colloquium — a joint Georgia Tech, Georgia State, and Emory series for related mathematical foundations.

Notes

Events are grouped by the current date in Atlanta (America/New_York). A dated event stays in the upcoming list through its final day, then moves into Past events automatically. Dates not yet announced remain separate; recurring seminar calendars do not expire.

The directory covers statistics, biostatistics, bioinformatics, ML, AI, and related research, mainly around Atlanta and nearby Georgia. It is selective rather than exhaustive. New announcements and changes are checked against official sources through a scheduled weekly review; these checks are separate from automatic date-based archiving. Consult the linked organizers for the latest dates, venues, registration, and eligibility. Historical entries retain their original edition even if an organizer later reuses its website.
