A collection of resources for research, teaching, and academic life at Georgia State University, together with career resources I find useful.

Research

  • GSU Library databases — find literature and subject databases through the university library.
  • Research Data Services — consultations and training in R, Python, data analysis, visualization, data management, and AI in research.
  • Interlibrary Loan — request articles and books that are not available in GSU’s collections.
  • University Research Services & Administration (URSA) — research support, proposal and award administration, training, and access to research systems.

Computing

For cluster access, storage, and R or Python batch jobs, see my GSU research computing guide.

  • ARCTIC — GSU’s advanced research computing services and infrastructure.
  • Elpis — manage research projects, computing allocations, project membership, and SSH certificates.
  • Hemera / Open OnDemand — access files, interactive applications, and cluster jobs through a browser.
  • ARCTIC documentation — instructions for access, Slurm jobs, software, storage, and data transfer.
  • ARCTIC consulting — help with research software, parallel computing, performance, and scaling workflows beyond a laptop.
  • ARCTIC workshops — training in cluster use and scientific computing; sign in to register.
  • GSU Technology Help — the university’s IT service portal for general technology support.
  • SAS OnDemand for Academics — browser-based SAS access through a separate SAS account.
  • sites@gsu — GSU-hosted websites; my GSU site is one example.

Grants and funding

  • Find funding / Pivot-RP — search funding opportunities, save searches, and explore potential collaborators using GSU’s institutional access.
  • GSU InfoReady — internal funding competitions and applications; consult each opportunity’s instructions.
  • Grantseeker and proposal resources — proposal-development materials, workshops, and resources for early-career researchers.
  • Brains & Behavior seed grants — support for neuroscience and behavior research. The posted 2026 application deadline was February 23, 2026; use the program page for future calls.
  • NVIDIA Academic Grant Program — external research support; current calls specify eligible topics, resources, and application dates.

Teaching and training

  • CETLOE — teaching consultations, course design, instructional technology, workshops, and teaching-related funding opportunities.
  • iCollege — GSU’s learning management system for course materials, assignments, and communication.
  • Library instruction and workshops — library instruction and research-skills training for courses and individual researchers.
  • Academic calendar — semester dates and exam schedules from the Office of the Registrar.

Faculty

  • Faculty resources — a central directory of faculty services, information systems, policies, and professional support.
  • New faculty orientation and onboarding — orientation information and the current onboarding checklist.
  • Faculty Handbook — university faculty policies and resources.
  • College of Arts & Sciences tenure-track guide — college guidance for tenure-track faculty reviews.
  • Promotion, tenure, and review — manuals, calendars, process information, and forms; some materials require GSU sign-in.
  • Digital Measures / Faculty Success — record and report teaching, research, and service activities.

Looking for events? The conference and seminar directory now has its own page: Statistics, ML & AI events.
